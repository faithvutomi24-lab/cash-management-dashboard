# Corporate Cash Management Dashboard

An Excel and Power BI prototype built for a self-directed Business Analysis case study on corporate cash management in banking.

The dashboard shows how a bank could give a business client one consolidated view of its accounts: total balance, balance by account, monthly cash in and cash out, and cash runway. All data is invented sample data for three mock accounts. It is not connected to any real bank.

Full case study: https://drive.google.com/file/d/11KniaGLvXr0WhlZJpbsBy3Gky1cAGNyQ/view?usp=sharing

Files:
- cash_management_data.xlsx: sample data and calculations
- cash_management_dashboard.pbix: the Power BI report
- Dashboard screenshot and the case study PDF

Limitations: sample data only, no real banking connection, no payment processing, no transfers between accounts.
